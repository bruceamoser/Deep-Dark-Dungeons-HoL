# Encounter challenge

Settings → Challenge sets Story, Adventurer (default/original balance), Veteran or Perilous for the current journey. Applies to newly generated floors and future camp wandering groups; existing floors/enemies are not regenerated. Saved journeys keep their choice; older saves default to Adventurer.

The encounter budget multiplier is 0.75/1/1.4/1.85, with group caps 4/5/6/7 and per-card repetition caps 1/2/3/4. This permits meaningful variation even when an early theme has only one eligible creature. Printed HP, attacks, defences and CV are unchanged. Wandering group size scales from the original two; fierceness/interruption odds do not change.

XP rules do not have a difficulty multiplier. Extra enemies can indirectly yield more encounter XP. Character progression pacing is a separate adaptation. Settings never retroactively modify fights or awarded levels.
