# Slower expedition progression

New heroes start at level1. Total XP thresholds are level2:10,000; level3:32,500; level4:85,000. Later level costs grow by 55%, rounded to 500 XP, up to the existing level30 cap. Awards for rooms, CV and recovered coins are unchanged. Difficulty has no direct XP multiplier, but extra enemies indirectly reward more.

Calibration sampled 30 generated seeds at depths1–10 with intended party levels1/2/3. At 70% of available room/encounter/chest XP, cumulative XP after floors3/6/10 averaged 9,579/32,380/85,784. This aims for level2 around depth4, level3 depth7, level4 depth11. These are pacing targets, not depth gates; clearing more, looting and risk choices affect advancement. Existing once-only discovery/kill/treasure claims remain.

Saves migrate to XP curve3 exactly once. Party, reserves and fallen heroes retain earned levels and proportional progress to their next level, re-expressed on the new XP scale. Trained choices, DP, inventory, HP, Grit and XP history are untouched. Older curve1 saves first retain the former curve2 earned-level migration. No level is removed and no healing/training occurs. A marker prevents repeat scaling; invalid curve2 XP records still reject.
