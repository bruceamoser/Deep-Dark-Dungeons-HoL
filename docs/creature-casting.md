# Creature casting audit — November 10, 2026

Inspected the full local Complete KayKit Collection v8 ZIP: Adventurers 2.0, Skeletons 1.1, Mystery Monthly series 4–6 and their character GLBs, including all fantasy and novelty bodies. Also inspected the purchased Dungeon Mason RPG Tiny Hero Wave Polyart Mesh directory: AllBodiesCloaks, humanoid head/accessory parts, weapons and the staged character catalog. It supplies humanoid customization, not authored animal/monster bodies. No new downloaded or purchased assets were committed.

Replaced the Monster Costume body for zombies/crypt ghouls with the ragged human Werewolf Man and corpse skin tint. Wights use the pale cloaked Vampire rather than skeleton mage. Goblin and orc casters use their own goblinoid family instead of Monstrosity. Goblins are now visibly smaller than orcs. Animated statues and clay helpers use stone/clay golem anatomy rather than bones or a robot; helpers are small. Glass sentinels use the crystalline Frost Golem silhouette. Native gallery covers every roster creature and screenshots all eight groups.

Skeletons retain authored Skeletons variants; human outlaws retain Survivalist/Ninja/Paladin/Witch; orcs retain Orc Raider/Brute; wolves, bats, ooze and small golems retain matching Quaternius animals/constructs already licensed in the game. Rats/spiders retain the existing anatomical adapters. Printed stats and encounter budgets are unchanged.

Limitations: neither purchased collection contains an exact kobold, rat-person or bear-person mesh. Kobolds retain small goblinoid proxies; wererats retain their existing lycanthrope proxy and werebears retain the broad Monstrosity. This audit improves mismatches where a better available body exists; those three families remain approximate, and exact anatomy needs another authored asset. Dungeon Mason human bodies would lose their animal anatomy further. No claim that every creature now has a unique authored mesh.
