# v0.5.0 — Builds worth descending for

Bruce's November 6 direction makes Legend a mechanical foundation, not a constraint
on game powers. The objective is deliberate four-hero tactics, frequent character
decisions, and loot that changes a build. This is the implemented first balance
pass, not a claim of campaign-length balance or final production art.

## Research translated into this game

- [Grim Dawn character basics](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/character/character-basics/),
  [Devotion](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/character/devotion/), and
  [item skills](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/character/item-skills/): layered character
  investment and equipment let several decisions reinforce a role. Here that becomes
  class signatures + DP powers + mastery + discipline-gated relics. We retain our
  four-character, alternating-turn combat rather than copying an action-RPG control model.
- [Last Epoch skills](https://lastepoch.com/skills/): specialization can change how a
  skill plays. Here a mastery point can reshape a learned power into a burst, increase
  condition pressure, trade movement range for an Action, or strengthen support.
- [Larian AI grid documentation](https://docs.larian.game/AI_grid_panel): surfaces
  participate in tactical interactions. Here water conducts lightning, freezing enables
  physical shatter, walls punish forced movement, and repeated severity conditions add.

These are design inferences from the linked primary sources, not copied abilities,
assets, names or rule text. No new third-party assets were introduced.

## Combat decisions, without encounter counters

All 225 game powers have no encounter/rest charge limits. Ordinary powers cost their
shown Action, Maneuver or Reaction. Supply actions still use ammunition, potions,
dressings or crafted devices. Healing/cleansing outside combat checks a field dressing;
magical wound removal consumes a restoration draught. HP and wounds remain distinct.

Action-granting support costs a Main Action, cannot queue multiple Maneuvers, and can
only restore an actually spent Reaction. One caster maintains one field of each kind;
overlapping fields of a kind affect an actor only once per six seconds. Buff magnitudes
use the strongest positive and negative values, and barriers use the strongest pool.
This bounds repetitive casting without reintroducing per-encounter bookkeeping.

Severity conditions add, including a second Burning 2 becoming Burning 4. Binary
conditions still refresh. Physical damage to a Freezing target consumes one temporary
stack and gains +2 damage, plus Shatter bonuses. Lightning against a target in water
arcs for 2 damage to other enemies within two squares, plus chain bonuses. A wall
collision causes 2 + unused forced distance physical damage before resistance;
Impact increases it. Vanish fades the model, adds a concealment ring and sheet badge,
and ends when the hero attacks or takes damage.

## Build layers

Every class has three signature choices at levels 1, 3 and 7: 90 signature designs.
Each combines an active power and passive role bonuses. Identical signature bonuses
use the strongest tier; choosing different roles lets a hero hybridize.

The Build forge has six branches and 30 talents: Steel & Impact, Ember & Ruin,
Winter & Wards, Storm & Motion, Blood & Shadow, and Life & Command. Heroes receive
two mastery points at level 1 and two more per trained level. A talent rank or a
specialized power costs one point. Each branch offers three basic nodes and two
mutually exclusive capstones. A capstone requires level 4 and three basic ranks in
that branch. Capstones include real tradeoffs such as damage for protection or
movement for armor. Refunds are free in town; no HP or Grit is restored.

Learned active powers offer three specializations appropriate to their effects.
Damage options change range, area or conditions; movement options change reach,
action cost or follow-up protection/damage; support options improve strength,
reach or duration. Support powers without an applicable numeric/duration effect
gain a self-ward instead, so a purchase never has no benefit. The action bar's
category, targeting radius and range use the specialized version.

Example directions: Impact Protector pins foes against walls; Ember Arcanist stacks
Burning and spreads it; Winter/Steel allies prime and shatter targets; Stormrunner
moves before attacking and uses wet terrain; Shadow ambushes and bleeds; Life/Command
heals while supplying barriers and movement. These are optional combinations,
not prescribed party compositions.

## Progression, equipment and feedback

The first earned level needs 400 XP, then each next level costs 175 more XP than the
last (level thresholds 0, 400, 975, 1725…). Exploration, encounters and recovered
coins still share XP among surviving expedition members. Train in town to claim
levels, DP and mastery. Existing saves retain their XP and can gain newly eligible
levels. A town Training & builds entry and prominent sheet Level up control expose
progression. Known disciplines and ranks remain visible alongside equipment.

All carried equipment, including creation grants, can be given to capable companions
outside combat. In the dungeon, they must be within two squares with a clear path.
Drop whole stacks during exploration into persistent ground caches; retrieving them
uses the ordinary world interaction/loot menu. Quantity and remaining supply die
travel with the item. The original creation receipt stays immutable but does not
respawn transferred/dropped items. Dropping recalculates carried bulk immediately.

Eight boss relics introduce build effects: Cinder Sovereign, Winter's Verdict,
Thunderwake, Last Bastion, Steps Between Worlds, Blood Debt, Black Thread Covenant,
and Chorus of the Unbroken. After these are exhausted, bosses generate other magic
gear. A persistent per-run name/relic registry prevents newly generated duplicates,
including after selling, losing, dropping, reload and merchant restock. Different
runs are independent. Existing duplicates are retained rather than deleting owned
items. Old saves cannot reconstruct items already lost before this registry existed.

Magic equipment has colored inventory frames, emissive equipped materials and angular
rune marks; relics receive a larger three-rune crest. Dormant items remain visibly
magical but dim, and still require the appropriate discipline for their effects.
These are equipment treatments on the current licensed models, not new bespoke models.

## Acceptance and remaining work

Automated checks cover all class/tier choices, at-will reuse, condition accumulation,
collision resistance/mastery, actual specialization effects, capstone/budget validation,
field supplies, build combinations, XP migration and real advancement, portable gear
save/reload, unique generation and relic discipline gating. Native interaction checks
cover the forge, purchases/refunds, training, transfers, ground recovery and visuals.

Further playtests should evaluate enemy time-to-kill, control-locking from high
condition stacks, healer action efficiency, optimal capstone combinations and relic
rarity over many expeditions. Enemy alternate actions, additional boss patterns,
more equipment silhouettes and final art/audio remain future production work.
