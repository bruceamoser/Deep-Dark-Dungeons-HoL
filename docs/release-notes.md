**v0.8.2 — A Circle in the Dark**

A playtest patch for dungeon camping, meaningful supplies, character presentation and clearer progression.

- **A fantasy soundtrack.** Approved music follows exploration, battles, Greyhaven and camp, with crossfades, repeating tracks, separate music/effects volumes and music mute. Camping uses Kevin MacLeod’s guitar-and-strings piece *Windswept*. Credits and CC BY 4.0 licence links are available in Sound.
- **Dungeon camps replace Safe Breath.** Plan at least eight hours, with six hours of sleep and two duties per hero in each eight-hour rota. Sleep restores 2 HP per hour and never restores Grit. Extend the camp when more recovery is needed. Workers keep watch; dedicated guards get a detection bonus. Wandering creatures can interrupt the camp, with completed hours and recovery preserved.
- **Healing and wound care.** Learned HP-healing skills add 4 HP per skill per care hour to a chosen companion, requiring a Field Dressing check. Wounds retain their recovery procedure and one treatment attempt per wound per campaign day. Severe treatment requires its existing supplies and care time. The compact planner shows current/projected HP, wounds and supplies over an animated campsite.
- **Camp duties and general foraging.** Cook, gather water, fletch arrows, make torches or dressings, forage, heal or stand guard. Production requires tools/materials and water needs a nearby source. General foraging uses visible d100 bands: a 50% baseline chance of one find, expanded for relevant training.
- **Growing supply bundles.** Combining matching nonempty consumables increases the larger supply die by one step, up to d20. For example, d4+d6 becomes d8; d8+d8 becomes d10. Extra components stay separate at d20. Bulk is preserved, and earlier combined reserves migrate once.
- **Supplies matter while exploring.** Water checks every 40 party movement steps, food every 80 and burning torches every 25. Camp/stationary time also consumes provisions and light; the UI shows upcoming checks. Counters survive saves. Small items use quarter Bulk and many supply bundles use half Bulk.
- **Appearance with an honest preview.** Show weapon, shield and backpack choices only for equipment the hero carries. Preview stowed choices immediately without equipping them. Fantasy-only accessory choices, named colour areas and clearer sections make customization easier to follow. Fixed magic-weapon identities remain intact.
- **Character and creature presentation.** Correct weapon roll while retaining the raised ready pose. Animated actors avoid incorrect frustum culling; untextured white humanoid fallbacks receive appropriate colours. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to rotate the dungeon or town camera.
- **Connected Forge, optional columns.** The Path of Exile-style connected tree remains the default. Wider spacing, collision-aware labels, highlighted connections and prerequisite/unlock lists improve readability. Column view is optional and uses the same abilities, costs and requirements.
- **Winding Greyhaven dirt paths.** Soft, uneven earth edges and curved lanes replace rigid square streets. The party and route preview follow the visible curves; service buildings and hotkeys remain available.

**Existing v0.6–v0.8 journeys continue.** Inventory, progression, injuries and spent Grit are preserved. Game adaptations are documented separately; the source book, Foundry repository and vendored HoL rules remain unchanged.

### Install or update

- **Debian/Ubuntu x64:** install `Deep-Dark-Dungeons-HoL-0.8.2-amd64.deb` with `sudo apt install ./Deep-Dark-Dungeons-HoL-0.8.2-amd64.deb`.
- **Other Linux x64:** download the AppImage or Linux bundle. Extract the bundle and run `bash install-linux.sh`. Restricted Ubuntu AppImage installations require a system Chrome/Chromium sandbox helper.
- **Windows x64:** run `Deep-Dark-Dungeons-HoL-Setup-0.8.2-x64.exe` (unsigned).

Installed releases can use **Updates → Save & restart to install**. Debian upgrades may request administrator authorization. The finished apps include licensed Dungeon Mason character art and KayKit scenery; public source-only builds use KayKit fallback characters unless purchased exports are staged. Purchased source and standalone models are not distributed. Some unusual cosmetic combinations can still overlap.
